What moves the price

Two quotes for the same house can be R50 000 apart. These are the usual reasons.

  • Battery size

    Usually the biggest single cost. A bill-saving system needs far less battery than whole-home backup.

  • Inverter type

    Hybrid inverters cost more than grid-tied but allow a battery and backup power.

  • Panels and mounting

    More panels cost more. Tile and slate roofs, and coastal wind ratings, add labour or hardware.

  • Wiring and the DB board

    Older homes may need electrical upgrades before solar can be connected safely.

  • Compliance

    City registration and the Certificate of Compliance should be included. Ask.

  • Brand and warranty

    Established brands cost more but come with longer, easier-to-claim warranties.

All solar and battery systems connected to the City of Cape Town grid must be registered and authorised as Small-Scale Embedded Generation (SSEG).

Before you accept a quote, check that it states

  • Panel, inverter and battery brands and models
  • Battery size in kWh and inverter size in kW
  • VAT included or excluded
  • City SSEG registration included
  • Certificate of Compliance included
  • Warranty terms in writing
  • Installation timeline
  • Payment schedule and deposit

Cost questions

Why do quotes differ so much?

Battery size, inverter brand, panel count, roof type and what is included (VAT, registration, compliance certificate) all change the total. Compare the same spec, not just the headline price.

Is there a government rebate for home solar?

There is currently no income-tax rebate for rooftop solar on a primary residence. The Section 12B allowance applies to businesses.

How long does solar take to pay back?

It depends on your usage, tariff and system. Our calculator gives an indicative range for your bill, but treat any fixed 'pays itself off in X years' promise with caution.

Is cheaper always worse?

Not always, but very low prices often leave out compliance, use small batteries or use lower-grade components. Ask what is excluded.

Do prices include VAT?

Not always. Ask each installer to state whether the quote includes VAT.
