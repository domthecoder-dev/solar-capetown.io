Solar quotes in Table View & Blouberg, Cape Town

Strong coastal wind and salt exposure are the main design factors. Open roofs with little shading give good solar potential when the installation is built for the conditions.

Suburbs we cover: Table View, Bloubergstrand, Milnerton, Sunningdale, Parklands, Melkbosstrand.

What to know in Table View & Blouberg

Wind-rated installation

Ask how panels are fixed and what wind rating the mounting meets.

Corrosion protection

Choose coastal-rated hardware and check the warranty does not exclude salt-air damage.

Municipal supply area

Most of these areas are supplied by the City of Cape Town, but confirm whether your property is City- or Eskom-supplied, as the registration route differs.

Indicative costs (October 2026)

  • Starter hybrid: R75 000 – R120 000
  • Family hybrid: R120 000 – R220 000
  • Large home / small business: R220 000 – R400 000

All solar and battery systems connected to the City of Cape Town grid must be registered and authorised as Small-Scale Embedded Generation (SSEG). See our full cost guide.
