What happens, step by step

  1. Step 1 · About a minute

    Estimate your savings

    Tell the calculator your monthly bill, which way your roof faces and whether you want backup. You get an indicative system size, cost range and monthly saving straight away. Nothing is sent to anyone yet.

  2. Step 2 · About a minute

    Tell us where you are

    Add your name, WhatsApp number, email and suburb. We ask whether you own or rent and when you want to install, so installers can prepare the right system. You tick a consent box so we can share your details.

  3. Step 3 · Usually within one business day

    We introduce up to 3 installers

    We pass your request to up to 3 installers who serve your suburb. They contact you by phone or WhatsApp. You can ignore any of them, and you decide who gets a site visit.

  4. Step 4 · Your pace

    Compare written quotes

    Most installers visit to check your roof and distribution board before quoting. Put the quotes side by side, ask questions, then pick one or none. You never owe us anything.

Have these ready and quotes come faster

You do not need any of it to request quotes, but installers will ask for it.

  • A recent electricity bill

    Shows your usage and tariff, so quotes are sized properly.

  • Photos of your roof and DB board

    Lets installers spot problems and give a firmer first price.

  • What you want solar to do

    Cut the bill, keep essentials on in an outage, or both.

  • Landlord or body-corporate OK

    If you rent or live in a complex, approval is usually needed first.

What we do, and what we don't

We do

  • Give you an honest, indicative estimate with the assumptions shown
  • Introduce you to up to 3 installers in your area
  • Tell you how we are paid: installers pay for introductions
  • Remove your details if you ask

We don't

  • Install, supply or guarantee any solar system
  • Charge you or take a deposit
  • Promise a specific price, saving or payback period
  • Sell your details to anyone beyond the installers you consent to

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Installers pay us for introductions, so it costs you nothing and you are never obliged to accept a quote.

Are you an installer?

No. We are a lead-matching service. We do not install, supply or guarantee any system, and we are not a party to your agreement with an installer.

How many companies will contact me?

Up to 3, based on your suburb. If fewer serve your area, you may hear from fewer.

Can I stay anonymous until I choose?

We need your contact details to introduce you, so installers will contact you. You can decline any of them.

What does an installer need to do for a legal installation?

Connected systems in Cape Town must be registered with the City, and the installation needs an electrical Certificate of Compliance. Ask every installer to confirm they will handle both.

What if I change my mind?

Message us on WhatsApp and we will remove your details and ask installers to stop contacting you.
