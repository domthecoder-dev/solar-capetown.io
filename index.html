Why Capetonians are going solar in 2026

Eskom has not load shed nationally since 16 May 2025. Localised load reduction and other outages can still occur. Many households still choose solar to cut their bill, protect against tariff increases and keep essentials running during faults and maintenance outages.

All solar and battery systems connected to the City of Cape Town grid must be registered and authorised as Small-Scale Embedded Generation (SSEG). A reputable installer will handle the application and issue a Certificate of Compliance. There is currently no income-tax rebate for rooftop solar on a primary residence. The Section 12B allowance applies to businesses.
